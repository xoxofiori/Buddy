# Buddy UI direction: our own identity (not a Talking Tom copy)

Talking Tom is a reference for **feel only**: a lovable character you care for. Do not copy its layout, its room-by-room swiping, its row of round need buttons, its level badge, its HUD or its sounds. Buddy's structure comes from **physical therapy**: recovery is the game.

## 1. One home scene: "Buddy's Backyard"
- A single cozy illustrated backyard instead of separate rooms: doghouse, food bowl, kiddie pool/bath tub, toy box, garden path.
- **Care actions happen by tapping objects in the scene** (tap the bowl → feed, tap the pool → bath, tap the doghouse → nap). There is no bottom button bar.
- The time of day follows the real clock: morning light, sunset glow, and night with stars and fireflies (Buddy gets sleepy).

## 2. Today's Plan card (top)
- A soft rounded card that slides down: "Today with Buddy" with 2–3 big exercise tiles plus the check-in. Each tile shows a mini Buddy doing the move, reps, and a big "Let's go" button.
- Done tiles flip over to show a sticker.

## 3. Recovery Trail (the progress system instead of levels)
- The garden path leads to a **trail map**: each day of exercises moves Buddy one step along it.
- Milestones unlock new places in Buddy's world: the park → the beach → the mountain. Each one adds a new backdrop and a new toy.
- The PT's plan length sets the trail length, so finishing the trail = finishing PT.

## 4. Bones + Buddy's Heart (instead of coins and 4 need bars)
- Reps earn **bones** (the currency). Perfect-form reps earn golden bones.
- Buddy's needs show as **one heart on his collar tag** that fills in sections (fed, clean, rested, played). Tap the tag to see the details.

## 5. Exercise = doing it *together*
- Split screen: Buddy demos on the left, the kid's camera with a skeleton on the right, and they move in sync.
- Buddy cheers each rep by name ("Nice one, Maya!"). Cute counters for reps (paw prints filling up).
- Finish → Buddy does a happy dance → bones fly into the bone jar.

## 6. Sticker Book (rewards + PT data)
- Milestones award collectible stickers ("7-day streak", "100 arm raises", "Brave check-in").
- The back pages of the sticker book are a kid-friendly progress chart that the PT/parents can view.

## 7. Check-ins as a ritual
- Morning: "Wake Buddy up" with a gentle tap, then "How's your knee today?" with a 5-face scale (Buddy mirrors the face the kid picks).
- Evening: "Tuck Buddy in" shows today's wins.

## Visual style
- Soft illustrated storybook look: flat colors with gentle gradients, rounded paper-cut shapes, subtle paper texture. Not glossy 3D game chrome.
- Palette from CLAUDE.md (Sky, Sprout, Coral, Sunny, Lavender, Cream, Deep Teal). SF Rounded. Big rounded cards with soft shadows.
- UI chrome stays minimal: the scene and Buddy are the interface.
